New Quizzes in Canvas: what is changing and what it means for you

The short version: Canvas is moving from Classic Quizzes to a newer quiz tool called New Quizzes. Goodwin plans to finish the move by Spring 2028. You do not have to do anything right now. If you would like to move your quizzes during this Course Copy, there is an optional way to do it, described below.

What is changing

Canvas has two quiz tools. Classic Quizzes is the original. New Quizzes is its replacement. A Canvas migration tool can convert a Classic quiz into a New Quiz.

About a quarter of our quizzes are already in New Quizzes.

Who it affects

Everyone who gives quizzes in Canvas, full-time and adjunct. Students will see a different quiz screen. The instructional design and LMS team will support you through it.

Why

Instructure, the company that makes Canvas, is moving Canvas toward New Quizzes and has said Classic Quizzes will eventually be retired. Supporting one quiz tool is simpler for you and for our team than supporting two.

Why now

Instructure has not set a retirement date. It has said we can expect at least a year of notice. We would rather move step by step, with help, than in a rush when a date arrives. So Goodwin is planning to finish by Spring 2028.

What is in it for you

New Quizzes offers more question types, more flexible accommodation settings for individual students, and improved analytics. Try one migrated quiz and decide whether they help you.

What to do now

Nothing is required. Your Classic quizzes will keep working as they do today.

If you would like your quizzes moved during this Course Copy, mark your course on the [tab name] tab of the Course Copy spreadsheet by [date]. We will do the migration and send you a short checklist to review before students see anything.

Good to know

  • Migration is one-way. A quiz moved to New Quizzes cannot be moved back to Classic.
  • New Quizzes does not yet match every Classic feature. If you rely on something specific, such as printing a quiz or searching your question banks, tell us. We are keeping a list and looking for workarounds.
  • We will keep a copy of your original course until the end of the term.

How to tell which quiz tool you are in

Look for the rocket icon next to a quiz's name in your course. The shape of the rocket tells you which tool that quiz uses.

If a quiz already shows the filled-in rocket, it is a New Quiz and needs nothing from you. If you are not sure which one you have, ask us at [email].
